## 1. Data stored only in your browser

The trainer saves your settings (for example weapon, magazine, sensitivity, practice mode and sound volume) and your score history (each saved run's score, with its date and time) in your browser's local storage, so they are still there on your next visit. This data stays on your device: we do not receive it.

This storage is strictly necessary for features you ask for, so it does not need consent. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser; that also deletes your score history.

## 2. Server logs

We use these logs to deliver the site, fix errors, and detect and stop abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a working and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). ## 3. Google Analytics, only with your consent

If you choose Accept in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4. This web analytics service is provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Without your consent, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

**What it collects:**

- the pages you view, and the interactions Google Analytics records by default (such as scrolling and clicks on links to other sites);
- time spent and the site you came from;
- device type, operating system, browser, screen size and language;
- approximate location (country, region and city), derived from your IP address.

Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. It sets cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_[ID]`) holding a random identifier, so that visits from the same browser can be counted together. They expire after [COOKIE LIFETIME, by default 2 years].

**Why:** to understand how the trainer is used, which features matter and where to improve it. We see aggregated reports only, and we do not use Google Analytics for advertising. **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). For the cookies, Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive and the national laws that implement it, such as § 25 TDDDG in Germany.

**Withdrawing consent:** open Cookie settings at the bottom of the page and reject Google Analytics. This stops further collection; you can also delete the cookies in your browser.

**Retention:** Google keeps the event data for [GA RETENTION, 2 or 14 months] and then deletes it. Google processes the data on our behalf under its data processing terms. Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## 4. Your consent choice

We remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics, so that we do not ask on every visit. The choice is stored on your device, in this site's local storage under `consent:analytics`, for 12 months; then the site asks again. Keeping a record of your choice is needed to respect it (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), and the storage is strictly necessary for that.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access your personal data (Art. 15);
- have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17);
- restrict its processing (Art. 18);
- receive it in a portable format (Art. 20);
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21);
- withdraw your consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), in particular in the country where you live or work.

We do not know who you are, so we may need details to find your data. For server logs, that is the time of your visit and your IP address. For analytics, it is the identifier stored in your `_ga` cookie, which lets us delete the data for that browser.

If you live in California: we do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising. You have the right to know, correct and delete it, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.
