How to practice

Set up once, then spray, read the result and go again.

  1. Turn off “Enhance pointer precision” in your mouse settings.
  2. Enter your sensitivity and ADS multiplier in Settings.
  3. Pick a weapon and a magazine.
  4. Press in the range and pull against the recoil until the magazine is empty.
  5. The closer your shots stay on the target, the higher the score, up to 100.
